The Paradigm Shift to Chiplets

To bypass monolithic reticle limits, semiconductor designers disaggregate large dies into smaller functional Chiplets reassembled at the package level, positioning FC-BGA substrates as the ultimate integration host.

3 Technical Bottlenecks in MCM FC-BGA Packages

  • Localized Hotspots: GPU logic dies consume >300W, generating intense thermal gradients that risk degrading adjacent heat-sensitive HBM stacks.
  • Non-Uniform Assembly Pressure: Bonding chiplets of varying silicon thicknesses risks uneven tilt and solder bridging.
  • Micro-Bump Alignment: Aligning hundreds of thousands of sub-55um micro-bumps across multiple chiplets demands sub-micron placement accuracy.
